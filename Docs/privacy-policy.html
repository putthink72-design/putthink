ScanPar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

ScanPar is a putting-guidance app. It uses your iPhone camera and LiDAR to scan the green between your golf ball and the hole, then estimates break and speed on this device.

1. What we process

  • Camera and LiDAR depth/mesh: to reconstruct the putting surface between ball and hole and to show live aiming overlays. We do not upload video, photos, or LiDAR meshes to our servers.
  • On-device scan files: height maps may be stored locally for that round’s guidance.
  • App settings: language preference is stored on the device.
  • Apple subscriptions: Apple processes payment through your Apple ID. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

2. Accounts and tracking

ScanPar does not create a user account. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. Permissions

Camera access is required to scan the green between the ball and the hole, lock those two points, and build the putting height map with LiDAR. If you deny camera access, scanning cannot run. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

4. Children

ScanPar is not directed at children under 13.

5. Your choices

Delete locally stored scans by deleting the app. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings (deleting the app does not cancel billing).

6. Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the ScanPar product page on the App Store.
